CONTEXT / MY ROLE

I delivered high-lift enhancements to the current LLM-powered search experience.

As a designer under the Chase Travel AI team, I worked cross-functionally to give our search experience a refresh. These enhancements focused on guiding the customer in natural-language searching and better communicating result relevance.

STRATEGY / IDENTIFYING THE PROBLEM

The connection between the search results and the customer intent was unclear.

Customers expressed frustrations with seeing irrelevant inventory, so how do we build trust with our customers by showing them inventory that accurately matches their search?

STRATEGY / THE STEPS I TOOK TO SOLVE IT

Who is disrupting and industry, and how? I compared design and AI strategies across top innovators such as Expedia, Tripadvisor, Perplexity, and Google Gemini.
