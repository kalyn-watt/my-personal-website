How it works

Three things happen, in order

DEPOSIT

Speak or write, unedited

Open Loam when you have thirty seconds or thirty minutes. Talk it out loud on a walk, or type it at your desk. No prompts, no formatting — just what's actually on your mind.

SETTLE

Loam reads across everything

Every entry stays private and unsorted on your end. Underneath, Loam is comparing today's entry to last month's, last season's — watching for what returns.

SURFACE

Themes rise, with the evidence

Once a pattern is real — not just one bad Tuesday — Loam names it and shows you the entries that built it, in your own words, so you can trust the read.
